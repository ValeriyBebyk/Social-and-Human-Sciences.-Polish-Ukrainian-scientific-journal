Publishing Process

Step-by-step guide for authors

1
Submission of the article

The author submits the manuscript according to the journal's requirements.

2
Initial Editorial Review

Checking for compliance with technical requirements and plagiarism check.

3
Peer Review (Double-blind)

Independent experts evaluate the scientific value of the work.

4
Revision (if needed)

The author addresses the reviewers' comments and submits a revised version.

5
Final Decision & Formatting

The Editorial Board makes the final decision on publication and prepares the layout.

6
Publication

The article is published in the current issue and assigned a DOI.
